The three problems

parser: day and month swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. “4 March turned into 3 April.” This is the bug #4471 names, and the fix queued for it covers only this row.

hint: the booking-v3 form. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads their date day first. Same symptom, a swap, but it only comes through the form at the three booking-v3 tenants, and the parser fix does not touch it.

one-day move: off by one day. These are no swap at all. A date moves by exactly one day, or two people in different time zones see different days for the same meeting. That points to time zone handling, not to date order. The desk had already listed them in desk-notes.json; the cause table filed them under parser by tenant, so nobody counted them.

#4471 is not "one issue, one fix queued". Shipping the parser fix leaves the other two rows open.

Why they were merged

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”), so subject matching cannot tell them apart.
  • Intake tags (meta.intakeTag) come from the reporter’s browser and disagree with the cause in both directions. The cause comes from the tenant: their locale, their booking form, and the channel the report came through.

What to do next time

  • Before merging into a big ticket, check the cause with the triage explorer, not the subject.
  • A report that is no swap (one day off, differs by place) is a different problem. Give it a ticket.
